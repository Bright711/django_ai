THE HISTORY
OF UNITED

1878–Today

THE THEATRE OF DREAMS

From Newton Heath to a global football institution, Manchester United's story is defined by ambition, resilience and a commitment to attacking football.

1878

Newton Heath LYR

The club is founded as Newton Heath LYR Football Club by railway workers in Manchester.

1902

A new name

The club becomes Manchester United and begins a new chapter in its history.

1958

The Munich air disaster

A defining moment that shaped the spirit and identity of the club and its supporters.

1968

European champions

Manchester United becomes the first English club to win the European Cup.

1999

The Treble

An historic Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League treble is completed.

2013

A 20th league title

The club surpasses the landmark of 20 English top-flight championships.
